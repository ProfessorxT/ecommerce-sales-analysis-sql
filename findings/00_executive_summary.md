# Executive Summary

**To:** Chief Marketing Officer / Leadership Team
**From:** Senior Data Analyst
**Subject:** Ecommerce Sales & Customer Analytics — Executive Findings

## Executive Recommendation

The business has demonstrated strong customer scale and transaction volume, but future growth should prioritize **customer retention and order-volume recovery rather than acquisition alone**. Customer value is highly concentrated among repeat/VIP customers, while a large first-time-buyer population represents the clearest opportunity for incremental growth.

## 1. Customer Economics Are Highly Concentrated

The business has **10,000 registered customers**, of whom **8,800 (88%) have placed an order**. However, **4,999 customers (49.99%) are one-time buyers**, generating only **12.42% of revenue**. In contrast, **2,614 VIP customers (26.14%) generate 78.19% of revenue**, with average customer spend of approximately **₹75,689**.

This creates a two-part growth priority: convert more first-time buyers into repeat customers while protecting the existing VIP revenue base.

## 2. Revenue Growth Is Primarily Volume-Driven

Across March–June 2026, paid-order product revenue reached approximately **₹23.00 Cr**. Revenue peaked in April at **₹10.43 Cr**, increasing **82.20% MoM**, before declining **40.85% in May** and **73.32% in June**.

Monthly AOV remained relatively stable at approximately **₹6.2K–₹6.4K**, indicating that the major driver of revenue volatility was **order volume rather than basket size**.

## 3. Growth Priorities for Q4

The analysis supports shifting marginal marketing investment toward **second-order conversion, lifecycle engagement, and VIP retention**. Priority analyses should next diagnose the causes of declining order volume across traffic, conversion, inventory, payments, and marketing channels.

The highest-value next step is moving from descriptive reporting toward **cohort LTV, repurchase propensity, and marketing incrementality**, enabling the business to allocate budget based on expected incremental customer value rather than acquisition volume alone.
